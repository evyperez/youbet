## Does your school or university want to attract international students?

YouBet! School supports educational institutions that want to expand their global presence and attract qualified international students.

We connect schools and universities with families and students interested in academic and cultural immersion programs, helping partner institutions strengthen their internationalization strategy.

### How we can help

- Promote your institution to our audience of students and families interested in studying abroad
- Support the process of featuring your school or university on our website
- Commercial and communication support to increase qualified lead generation
